💡 Disaster Recovery Guide (What if the PC dies?)

If this computer ever breaks down or is replaced:
1. Install the app on the new Windows PC.
2. Copy your latest textile_backup_...sqlite file from your USB flash drive.
3. Rename it to inventory.sqlite and place it inside the data/ folder.
All your sales history, debts, and inventory will immediately be restored.
